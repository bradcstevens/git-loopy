Release-line promotion prototype

Question: Does separating label-driven prerelease advances from Promotion let a closed vX.Y.Z milestone cut only its matching stable Release, while a major Bump class is exempt and the next line starts fresh?

Current Release line

Free play

Closing a normal issue never receives milestone state. A major closes its stable Release immediately.

Guided walkthroughs

Milestone promotion: close a MINOR issue, then close its matching target milestone. The prerelease was formed without reading any milestone; the separate tracker event promotes it.

Major exemption: close a MAJOR issue. It cuts stable without a milestone event.

Fresh line: finish a milestone Promotion, then close a PATCH issue. It starts at dev.1 from the new stable Release.
